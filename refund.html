Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

1. Digital service — try the free trial first

FamilyGuard is a digital subscription. That is why every new account gets a 3-day free trial — please test all features during the trial before purchasing.

2. When a refund is granted ✅

  • Technical failure: payment completed but the subscription did not activate (for 24+ hours) — full refund
  • Double payment: two payments for the same plan by mistake — the extra payment is refunded in full
  • Requests must be made within 7 days of payment (by email, with the payment ID)

3. When a refund is not granted ❌

  • You used the free trial, purchased a plan, and then changed your mind
  • Features not working due to child-device settings (battery optimization, notification access off) — fixes are provided in the install guide
  • Account suspended for violating the Terms (e.g. monitoring adults)
  • Most of the plan period has already been used

4. Cancellation

Plans do not auto-renew — no separate cancellation is needed. Plans simply expire and nothing further is charged. You may also delete your account entirely (which also removes your data).

5. How to request a refund

Email [email protected] with the subject "Refund", including:

  • Your login email + Razorpay Payment ID (pay_...)
  • A 2–3 line description of the problem

Verified requests are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method. This policy is issued under applicable Indian consumer law and worldwide consumer-protection norms.
