FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is FamilyGuard legal and ethical to use?

FamilyGuard is a parental control tool intended for parents or legal guardians of minor children. The child app is installed with one-time physical access to the device, and the product is designed for supervision, safety and guidance — not for monitoring adults or anyone without authority. Misuse of the app for spying on other individuals is against our terms.

Does my child know the app is installed?

The app is installed openly on the child's device as part of the family setup. We encourage parents to have an honest conversation with their children about healthy device habits — the tool works best as a support for trust, not secrecy.

Which devices are supported?

Any Android 7.0+ phone or tablet for both parent and child apps. The parent dashboard connects over the internet — check on your child from anywhere.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major methods via Razorpay: UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, all banking apps), debit & credit cards, and netbanking. Activation is automatic within about a minute of payment.

What happens after my free trial ends?

Your account moves to a free tier with limited weekly usage — no features are removed, and your data stays intact. You can subscribe anytime to unlock unlimited access.

Built for responsible parenting. FamilyGuard is designed for the supervision of minor children by their parents or legal guardians.
