Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

1. Overview

FamilyGuard is a parental control tool intended solely for parents and legal guardians supervising their minor children. This policy explains what data we collect, where it is stored, how long it is kept, and the rights you have over it.

2. Data we collect

  • Parent account: email address (for login), subscription status and expiry
  • Child device status: battery level, network type, online/offline state, list of installed apps
  • Activity data (while monitoring is enabled): notifications, chat notifications (app, contact, message), app usage time, camera photos (only on parent request), and Live View streaming (only on parent request)
  • Payment data: processed by Razorpay — we store only the payment ID and plan. Card/UPI details never reach our servers

We never sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

3. Where data is stored

All data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore). All transfers are encrypted over HTTPS/TLS.

4. Data retention

  • Photos: deleted immediately after being shown on the parent dashboard
  • Notifications, chats and alerts: automatically deleted after 24 hours
  • Account data: kept until the account is deleted (or upon email request)

5. Legal framework — India & worldwide

This service is operated in accordance with applicable data protection laws in India and worldwide:

  • India: Information Technology Act, 2000 (IT Act) and the IT (Reasonable Security Practices) Rules, 2011; Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)
  • European Union / UK: GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) — lawful basis is the parent's consent and legitimate interest in supervising their minor child
  • United States: COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) — verifiable parental consent is obtained before any collection from a child's device
  • Worldwide: users must comply with their local monitoring and privacy laws

6. Consent & lawful use

Installing the app on the child's device requires one-time physical access, performed by the parent/guardian. Monitoring is legal only for your own minor children. Monitoring adults or any person without their consent is unlawful and against our Terms.

7. Your rights

  • Delete your account at any time to remove all associated data
  • Request data deletion by email: [email protected]
  • Monitoring features (camera, live view, mic) operate only on explicit parent action

8. Contact

For any privacy-related question or request: [email protected] — we aim to respond within 48 hours.
