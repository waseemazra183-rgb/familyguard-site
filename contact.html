🔧 Common problems — instant fixes

  • App won't install (Play Protect block)? → See the Install Guide in the "Get Apps" section of this site
  • Paid, but Premium not activated? → Tap 🔄 Refresh on the dashboard and wait 2 minutes; if it still fails, email us with the Payment ID
  • No notifications coming through? → Open the app on the child phone → Permissions Hub → turn ON Notification Access
  • Camera not working? → On the child phone, set the Battery Health app to "Don't optimize" (battery settings)
  • Want to run a self-test? → Log in to the dashboard and tap the 🩺 Doctor button — it diagnoses and reports the issue automatically

⏱️ Response times

  • General questions: 24–48 hours
  • Payment issues: priority — usually the same day
  • Refund requests: 5–7 business days (after verification)
